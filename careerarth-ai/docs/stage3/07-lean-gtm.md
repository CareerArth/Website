# Stage 3.7 — Lean GTM (First 10–50 Users)

Scoped deliberately small: this is a channel-selection exercise for the Stage 4 pilot
cohort, not a marketing plan. No budget, headcount, or paid-acquisition plan is proposed.

## Channel ranking

| Channel | Cost | Speed | Likely effectiveness for this ICP | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Referrals from existing CareerArth contacts/clients** | ~Free | Fast | High — the ICP (stagnating mid-career professionals) is exactly who existing consultation clients or their peers already are; warm intro skips the trust problem entirely | **1** |
| **Founder/team direct outreach (LinkedIn, personal network)** | ~Free | Fast | High for the first 10–20 — 1:1 outreach to specific mid-career contacts matching the ICP, framed as "we built something, want your honest read" | **2** |
| **University/alumni networks** | Low | Medium | Medium — good for the *early-career* segment, weaker fit for this Stage 3 ICP (5–10 yrs experience) who are less connected to their alumni network day-to-day; useful for a secondary cohort, not the primary pilot | 3 |
| **Career communities (Reddit, niche Slack/Discord/WhatsApp groups)** | ~Free | Medium | Medium — high volume but low targeting precision and possible skepticism toward AI-scored tools posted by an unknown account | 4 |
| **Partnerships (HR/L&D teams, alumni associations, coaching networks)** | Low–Medium (relationship time) | Slow | Potentially high longer-term, but requires a pitch and lead time this size of pilot doesn't need yet | 5 |
| **Content (blog/LinkedIn posts about the ARTH framework)** | Low (time only) | Slow | Low for a 10–50 user pilot specifically — content compounds over months, not the right tool for an immediate small cohort | 6 |

## Recommended sequence for the first 10–50

1. **First 10:** direct outreach to specific people the team already knows who fit the
   ICP (stagnating, 5–10 yrs experience). Ask for a real diagnostic run + structured
   feedback in exchange for a free consultation. This doubles as the Stage 4 pilot
   recruitment.
2. **Next ~20:** referral ask built into step 1 — every pilot participant is asked
   "who else do you know in a similar spot?" This is the cheapest and highest-trust
   channel available and requires no new infrastructure.
3. **Remaining ~20:** one or two career-community posts (Reddit/LinkedIn), framed as
   "we're validating a new tool, looking for honest testers," explicitly not as a sales
   pitch — consistent with treating this as a Stage 4 validation exercise, not a launch.

Deliberately excluded at this stage: paid ads, SEO/content investment, formal
partnerships — all are premature before Stage 4 shows the diagnostic-to-consultation
conversion rate is real.
