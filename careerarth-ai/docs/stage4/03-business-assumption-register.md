# Stage 4.3 — Business Assumption Register

Ranked by (risk if wrong) × (how cheap it is to get real signal) — highest priority
items are both consequential and answerable with the smallest pilot in
[04-pilot-protocol.md](04-pilot-protocol.md).

| # | Assumption | Why it matters | Current evidence | Risk if wrong | Cheapest validation | Success threshold | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A real Claude-generated report is meaningfully better than the mock template a real user would notice | The entire "AI-personalised analysis" value prop depends on this; it has literally never been observed (all runs are `generator: mock`) | None — zero real generations exist | If real output isn't clearly better, the core value prop collapses to "a form with a fixed template" | Run 3–5 real personas through the pipeline with a live API key, read the output personally before recruiting anyone | Team agrees, on read, the report is specific/non-generic enough to show a stranger | **1** |
| 2 | Stagnation-driven, mid-career professionals recognize themselves in the report and find it credible | This is the chosen ICP ([Stage 3 §1](../stage3/01-icp-customer-problem.md)); if the report doesn't land with them specifically, the beachhead choice is wrong | None — no real user has read any report | Wrong ICP wastes the entire GTM plan on the wrong audience | Pilot post-survey Q1–Q3 (accuracy, specificity, trust) | Avg ≥ 3.5/5 on accuracy and trust | **2** |
| 3 | Free diagnostic → paid consultation conversion is high enough to sustain Model B | Direct input to the unit-economics model ([Stage 3 §4](../stage3/04-pricing-unit-economics.md)); base case assumes 10% | None — the production funnel's shape exists but its real conversion rate was not examined here, and this MVP's own funnel has zero completions logged | If conversion is materially below ~5%, Model B's contribution margin goes negative or near-zero at any reasonable price | `consultation_interest` events ÷ `report_viewed` events, already instrumented (`src/metrics.ts`) | ≥ 30% interest rate among pilot participants (stated target, not proof of paid conversion) | **3** |
| 4 | Real users can complete the 20-item questionnaire without excessive drop-off or confusion | Product viability — a diagnostic nobody finishes has no funnel to optimize | Zero `intake_completed` events logged anywhere, ever, by anyone | High drop-off kills the funnel before the report even matters | `intake_completion_rate` + per-step drop-off, already instrumented | ≥ 80% completion among pilot participants | **4** |
| 5 | People will state a realistic willingness to pay for the consultation, and it clears a viable price floor | Determines whether any price in the Stage 3 pricing table is realistic | None — all Stage 3 price points are market guesses | If real WTP is far below ₹1,999, either the price, the packaging, or the whole model needs rethinking | Direct WTP question in pilot survey (open-ended, unanchored) | Median stated value ≥ ₹1,500 | **5** |
| 6 | The ARTH score's heuristic weighting reads as credible/useful despite being explicitly unvalidated | Positioning ([Stage 3 §2](../stage3/02-value-proposition-positioning.md)) depends on honesty about this *not* undermining trust | None | If disclosing "not scientifically validated" tanks trust, the positioning statement itself needs revision, not just the product | Pilot post-survey Q3 (trust), interview Q2 (disagreement moments) | Avg trust ≥ 3.5/5 despite disclosure | **6** |
| 7 | Consultant review time is short enough (~15–30 min) to make Model B/C consultant economics viable | Stage 3's unit economics ([Stage 3 §4](../stage3/04-pricing-unit-economics.md)) treats consultant time as the dominant cost lever | One QA data point: 6.5 minutes, by the developer, on a synthetic profile with no real edits needed — not representative of a real consultant under real stakes | If real review takes 45–60+ min, contribution margin in the conservative pricing scenario goes negative | Time a real consultant (even the team, acting deliberately as a first-time reviewer) reviewing a *real* Claude-generated report | ≤ 20 min average, consistent with Stage 3's base-case assumption | **7** |
| 8 | The 18-role knowledge base covers enough of the pilot cohort that "out-of-corpus" degradation is rare | Report quality and credibility depend on the KB actually matching real profiles, not just the 6 hand-picked personas | Personas were deliberately written to fit the KB; no adversarial/real-world test exists | Frequent out-of-corpus fallback would make early users feel the product doesn't know their field | Track `currentRoleMatched`/`targetRoleMatched` (already in `retrieval` output) across pilot profiles | ≥ 80% of pilot profiles get a matched current role | **8** |
| 9 | CareerArth's existing production funnel (free diagnostic → consultation CTA) actually converts at a non-trivial rate today | Stage 3's Model B recommendation partly rests on "this motion already exists and works," which was asserted from the page copy, not measured | Page copy confirmed to exist; no analytics reviewed | If the *existing* funnel already converts poorly, adding an AI diagnostic doesn't fix a demand problem | Pull real analytics/CTA-click data from the production site (outside `careerarth-ai/`, but cheap — no pilot needed) | Establishes a real baseline, no fixed threshold | **9** |
| 10 | A real user perceives CareerArth as meaningfully different from asking ChatGPT directly | Central to the differentiation claim in [Stage 3 §6](../stage3/06-competitive-analysis.md) | None — the competitive analysis is the team's own reasoning, not user-tested | If users don't perceive a difference, willingness to pay for anything beyond "free chatbot" collapses | Interview Q3 ("would you tell a friend this was worth their time") | Majority of interviewees name a specific reason it beat a general chatbot | **10** |

Two items intentionally excluded from this top-10 despite being real gaps (both already
named in the evidence audit): long-run referral/repeat-use behavior, and payment-flow
conversion (no payment flow exists to test). Both require infrastructure this capstone
is explicitly not building; they belong in the post-90-day roadmap, not this register.
