# CareerArth AI — Executive Summary

**Aryan Bhandari · Entrepreneurship Capstone · Submitted to Professor Tushar Jaruhar**

**Opportunity.** Mid-career professionals (5–10 years in) who have quietly stalled face a
gap between vague worry and expensive commitment: ChatGPT gives unstructured advice, a
coach demands a big engagement before the problem is even named. CareerArth — an existing
career-advisory brand whose site already runs a free-assessment → paid-consultation
funnel — can own the structured first step in between. *(ICP and opportunity: reasoned
hypotheses, not yet customer-validated.)*

**Solution.** CareerArth AI turns a 5–7 minute structured intake into a deterministic
**ARTH scorecard** — Alignment, Risk Exposure, Trajectory, Human Capital (a prototype
diagnostic framework, not a validated psychometric instrument) — plus an AI-drafted,
evidence-cited analysis grounded in a curated 18-role career knowledge base: matched
career paths, skill gaps, a 90-day plan, and a consultation call-to-action. Three design
rules protect trust: the AI never computes scores, every claim must cite its evidence
(enforced by an automated lint), and a human consultant reviews before anything is final.

**MVP — demonstrated.** A working branded web product: intake wizard → scorecard →
report → consultant review screen → funnel instrumentation. **42/42 automated tests
pass.** Six synthetic personas produce genuinely differentiated results (overall scores
42–88, four interpretation bands, distinct career-path sets, clean handling of a
career-break profile). *Boundary: these personas are QA fixtures, not customer evidence —
and every report to date uses a deterministic mock generator; the Claude integration is
fully implemented but has never run live (no API key), so real AI report quality is
unobserved.*

**Business model — hypothesis.** Free diagnostic as lead generation; the paid human
consultation (hypothesised ₹1,999–₹4,999) is the revenue event — formalising the motion
the production site already runs. Modelling shows estimated AI cost ($0.05–$0.15/report,
estimated, never measured) is a rounding error; **consultant time and free→paid
conversion dominate the economics** (base-case contribution ≈ ₹2,089/customer at an
assumed 10% conversion — every input an explicit assumption).

**Evidence line.** Demonstrated: technical feasibility of the full workflow.
Implemented-but-unvalidated: live Claude generation and its cost. Untested: demand,
conversion, willingness to pay, pricing, product-market fit. No real customer has used
the product; nothing here claims otherwise.

**Next step.** A ready-to-run 10–20-person pilot — recruitment message, consent, surveys,
interview guide, WTP questions, and pre-committed success/failure thresholds (≥80% intake
completion; trust ≥3.5/5; ≥30% consultation interest; median WTP ≥₹1,500) — requiring
**zero new engineering**, because the MVP already instruments every funnel step. Then a
30/60/90-day roadmap ending in an evidence-based **continue / revise / pivot / stop**
decision.
