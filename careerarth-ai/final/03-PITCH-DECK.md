## Slide 2 — Problem

**Careers don't collapse. They erode.**

- Mid-career professionals stall quietly — solid reviews, no movement
- They feel it, but can't decompose it: skills? industry? network? ambition?
- Their options are extreme (quit / do nothing) or expensive (hire a coach, unbriefed)

*Notes: "Gilded stagnation" — the framing the CareerArth brand already markets. The pain is epistemic: they can't name the problem.*

---

## Slide 3 — Customer

**Beachhead: the stalled mid-career professional**

- 5–10 years in · no promotion or scope change in 2+ years
- Tech, finance, marketing/consulting (what our knowledge base covers)
- Trigger: denied promotion · a peer's title change · a tenure milestone
- **Hypothesis:** won't pay for a quiz — will pay for a consultation once the diagnostic proves it understands them

*Notes: Chosen over early-career (low urgency, low WTP) and switchers (stresses our thinnest asset). It's also what the rubric measures best — tenure penalty on Trajectory.*

## Slide 4 — Alternatives

**Everything they try today fails a different way**

| Option | Failure mode |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Fluent, unstructured, ungrounded |
| Career coach | Big commitment before the problem is named |
| LinkedIn | A feed, not a decision |
| Personality tests | Traits, not their situation |
| Free content | They do all the synthesis |

*Notes: The gap isn't "AI advice" — that's free. The gap is a cheap, structured, trustworthy first step owned by someone who sells the next step.*

---

## Slide 5 — Solution

**7 minutes → a scored, evidence-cited career diagnostic → a human**

1. Structured intake (profile + 20-item questionnaire)
2. Deterministic ARTH scorecard — **no AI in the numbers**
3. AI analysis grounded in a curated career knowledge base — **every claim cites its evidence**
4. Career paths, skill gaps, 90-day plan
5. **Human consultant reviews before anything is final**

*Notes: The three bolded rules are the trust architecture — and the answer to "why not just ChatGPT?"*

---

## Slide 6 — The ARTH Framework

**Four questions, one scorecard**

- **A**lignment — does today's work build toward the ambition? (×0.30)
- **R**isk Exposure — how insulated from industry/automation shifts? (×0.25)
- **T**rajectory — options expanding or narrowing? (×0.20)
- **H**uman Capital — network, brand, transferability? (×0.25)

Five bands: Strong · Stable · Watch · Vulnerable · Critical

**A prototype structured framework — not a validated psychometric instrument. Disclosed on every report.**

*Notes: Honesty is the positioning. The weights are heuristic and auditable, not hidden behind AI fluency.*

## Slide 7 — Working MVP

**Built, tested, instrumented**

- Branded web flow: intake → scorecard → report → consultant review
- **42/42 automated tests pass**
- 18-role curated knowledge base (O*NET-sourced), deterministic retrieval
- Full funnel instrumentation, ready for a pilot
- 6 synthetic personas: scores 42→88, four bands, distinct path sets

**Boundary: all reports to date are mock-generated. Claude integration is implemented, never yet run live. Synthetic personas are QA, not customers.**

## Slide 8 — Business Model

**Free diagnostic → paid consultation**

- The diagnostic is lead generation, not the product
- Revenue event: human consultation — hypothesised **₹1,999–₹4,999**
- Formalises the funnel the CareerArth site already runs
- Phase 2 option: premium AI + human-reviewed strategy bundle — after conversion evidence

*Notes: Model chosen from three alternatives; it fits both the MVP's real delivery order and the trust posture (no payment before value shown).*

---

## Slide 9 — Unit Economics (hypotheses)

**The AI is a rounding error. The human is the cost.**

- Estimated AI cost: **$0.05–0.15/report** (estimated — never measured live)
- Base case: ₹2,999 price · 10% assumed conversion · 45-min session → **≈ ₹2,089 contribution/customer**
- Conservative case survives (≈ ₹194); upside ≈ ₹4,453
- **Margin hinges on conversion rate + consultant minutes — both testable in a pilot**

*Notes: Every input is a labelled assumption. The structural insight is real: optimise conversion and consultant time, not tokens.*

## Slide 10 — Differentiation

**No tech moat. A trust-and-distribution bet.**

- Copyable: the pipeline, the rubric, the prompts — weeks of work for any team
- Hard to copy: an existing advisory brand + funnel · AI paired with **real** human review · evidence-cited discipline
- Durable asset must be *built*: funnel data, review learnings, outcome tracking

*Notes: Deliberately anti-hype. Faculty should hear: this founder knows exactly what is and isn't defensible.*

## Slide 12 — Validation & Next Steps

**The pilot is ready to run — zero new engineering**

- 10–20 real users: consent → diagnostic → report → surveys → WTP → interview
- Pre-committed thresholds: ≥80% completion · trust ≥3.5/5 · ≥30% consultation interest · median WTP ≥₹1,500
- 30/60/90 days: real Claude reports → pricing anchors + consultant timing → **continue / revise / pivot / stop** memo

**Ask: permission to treat this as a live venture experiment, not a finished claim.**
